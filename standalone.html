3 · Calibrations and tools

Source correction: calibrate against a known blackbody

Measure a lamp of known colour temperature (tungsten halogen). A per-band multiplier makes the instrument show that shape; it applies to later light measurements while the switch above is on.

Absolute level (brightness / power)

The factory scale is assumed to be mW/m²/nm and is unchecked. One factor scales every later light measurement. Use the same exposure settings and geometry as for the real measurements.

Blackbody at several temperatures (find the true temperature)

Vary the lamp's temperature and add each step (3 or more). Steps are determined from the data alone; the absolute value needs the true temperature of one step (pyrometer, filament resistance). Without it the factory colour calibration and a grey emitter are trusted.

Calibration data (EEPROM)
